About

A quiet standard, held since 2016.

Zarah Express was founded in Sandton in 2016 to answer a narrow question well: how does a guest, an executive or a delegation move through a city without ever having to think about it?

The answer has never been complicated. Premium German vehicles, kept immaculate. Chauffeurs who are trained, vetted, discreet and impeccably presented. A control room that knows where every car is and where it needs to be next. And a habit of arriving early, so nobody else has to hurry.

Ten years on we serve five-star hotels and their concierge desks, JSE-listed corporates, event and roadshow producers, and private clients who value being known rather than processed. We are a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor, based at the corner of Sloane and Main in Bryanston — minutes from the country's densest concentration of head offices, and a clean run to O.R. Tambo.

We are not a shuttle company. Our clients do not book a seat; they engage a service.

What we hold ourselves to

Discretion as policy

Chauffeurs sign confidentiality undertakings. What is said in the car, and who was in it, stays there.

Early, not on time

Fifteen minutes is the standard. Punctuality is the only luxury that cannot be bought back.

One point of contact

No call centre. A named person who already knows your account and your preferences.

Accountable to procurement

Level 1 B-BBEE, consolidated invoicing and per-trip reporting that stands up to an audit.
