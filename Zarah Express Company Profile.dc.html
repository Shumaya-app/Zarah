Who we are 01

A quiet standard, held for ten years.

Zarah Express was founded in 2016 to answer a narrow question well: how does a guest, an executive or a delegation move through a city without ever having to think about it?

The answer has never been complicated. Premium German vehicles, kept immaculate. Chauffeurs who are trained, vetted, discreet and impeccably presented. A control room that knows where every car is and where it needs to be next. And a habit of arriving early, so that nobody else has to hurry.

Today we serve five-star hotels and their concierge desks, JSE-listed corporates, event and roadshow producers, and private clients who value being known rather than processed. We are a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor, based at the corner of Sloane and Main in Bryanston, Sandton — minutes from the country's densest concentration of head offices, and a clean run to O.R. Tambo.

We do not describe ourselves as a shuttle company. Our clients do not book a seat; they engage a service.

What we do 02

Six services.
One standard.

Every engagement is quoted, confirmed in writing and tracked to completion. Standing requirements move to a corporate or hotel account with monthly consolidated billing.

i

Airport transfers

Flights monitored, meet-and-greet inside the terminal, luggage handled. Punctual pickups and drop-offs that start and end a stay properly.

ii

Executive chauffeuring

Half-day, full-day and at-disposal engagements for directors and visiting principals. A car and chauffeur who stay with you all day.

iii

Hotel & guest transport

A dedicated line for concierge desks: VIP arrivals, in-city transport, shuttles for guests and staff, luggage assistance.

iv

Roadshows & events

Multi-car itineraries run to the minute, with one point of contact for the whole programme — conferences, results roadshows, delegations.

v

Staff & group transport

Scheduled runs in the V-Class for teams, site visits and late shifts — the same vehicles and vetting as our executive work.

vi

Logistics support

Time-critical documents, equipment and sensitive parcels moved discreetly, tracked and signed for.
